Conversation settings

Everything said here is addressed to the people in it, so a conversation notifies on every message by default. Mentions only is for a large group that has become a busy room; in a one-to-one it means the other person has to type your name to reach you, which is rarely what anyone wants. Muting keeps the unread count without the interruption.

You'll stop receiving messages from this group and it will leave your sidebar. What you wrote stays — the people still here were part of the conversation too. Someone in the group has to add you back.
