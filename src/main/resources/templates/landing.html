ThreadOrbit

Team chat you run yourself — channels, threads and direct messages, on your identity provider and your database.

  • Threads that stay readable — replies live in the thread instead of scrolling the channel out from under everyone else.
  • Search that finds it — full-text search across every channel you can read, with permalinks that open the surrounding conversation.
  • Markdown, files and polls — code blocks, streamed attachments, reactions, mentions, reminders and /poll.
  • Single sign-on — authentication is your Keycloak realm, so there's no second password to manage or leak.
